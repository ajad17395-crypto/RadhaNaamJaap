Privacy Policy

Welcome to Shree Radha Naam Jap.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

Shree Radha Naam Jap is designed to work completely offline.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information such as:

Offline Data

Your jap count, settings, themes, and preferences are stored only on your device.

This information never leaves your phone.

Advertisements

If advertisements are enabled, the app may display ads using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect certain non-personal information such as:

Internet Connection

The application does not require the internet for its main devotional features.

Internet is used only for:

  • Loading advertisements (if enabled)

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions necessary for its features.

No unnecessary permissions are requested.

Children

The application is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
